# Poll cadence matched to the feed

- Collector polls every 10 s by default; repeated/older snapshots from UTA's second server are skipped.
- GPS history sampled to one ping per vehicle per 30 s.
- Live map refreshes every 10 s.
- Rationale: see ADR [2026-09-21-2108-poll-cadence](../adr/2026-09-21-2108-poll-cadence.md)
