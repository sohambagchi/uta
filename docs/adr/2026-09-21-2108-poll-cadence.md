# Poll every 10 s to match UTA's feed cadence

**Status:** accepted

**Context:** Measured 2026-09-21 over 4 min: both feeds get a new snapshot every 10–11 s (`cache-control: no-store`), vehicles report every ~10 s, and fetches take ~0.4 s. Two out-of-sync servers answer the URLs, so a fetch can return the previous snapshot (header timestamp goes backwards by 1–12 s). The collector polled every 30 s, dropping 2 of 3 snapshots and leaving the final prediction for a stop up to 30 s stale, which is what the observed window filters on.
**Decision:** Collector default interval 10 s (`collector.POLL_INTERVAL_S`). A snapshot whose header timestamp isn't newer than the last processed one is skipped (`PollState`). GPS history keeps one ping per vehicle per 30 s (`POSITION_EVERY_S`), so storage stays as before. The dashboard's live map refreshes every 10 s.
**Alternatives:** Poll faster than 10 s (only re-reads the same snapshot); keep 30 s (stale observed times, jumpier map).
**Consequences:** 3× the stop-event upserts (~3.5k rows per poll, ~1 s per poll). The observed lead window was calibrated at 30 s polling and should be rechecked against GPS once there's a day of 10 s data.
