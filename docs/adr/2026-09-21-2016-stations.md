# Stations: group stops by normalized name within 250 m

**Status:** accepted

**Context:** UTA's GTFS has no `parent_station`. Bays of a hub and the two sides of a street are separate stops, so the same place appears several times, and two routes that meet at a place use different stop_ids.
**Decision:** `stations` table, with `stops.station_id` set to the lowest member stop_id. Two stops are in the same station when their names match after normalization (parentheticals removed, punctuation and case folded, West/North/... abbreviated) and they are within 250 m. `route_stops` orders each route direction's stations by merging trip patterns, most frequent first.
**Alternatives:** Pure distance clustering. Rejected: it merges different intersections downtown.
**Consequences:** 5,423 stops become about 3,290 stations. Station ids depend on stop_ids, which UTA keeps stable. Everything is re-derived on every GTFS import.
