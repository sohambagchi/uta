Pick two stops to see how long the trip takes. You'll see the average, how it varies by time of day and day of week, and how each route that connects them compares.

Scheduled times come from UTA's GTFS timetable. Observed times come from the realtime feed, recorded by the collector since it started running.

Schema

SQLite, read-only here. Times are epoch seconds; use datetime(x,'unixepoch','localtime'). Local time is America/Denver.
